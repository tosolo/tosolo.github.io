How it works

Focused on the thread you’re viewing.

FileTracker reads only the Gmail thread you currently select. It does not modify or delete email, and uses its access solely to help you find and reopen the materials you need.

  • Uses read-only Gmail access
  • Downloads attachments only when you choose
  • Shows sender and date details for the current thread
  • Takes you directly to the original email

Privacy first

Your email content is never stored on Tosolo servers.

Your emails, attachments, images, links, and search results are processed locally on your device and are not sent to or stored on Tosolo servers. FileTracker stores only minimal preferences, such as the dark mode setting, in Chrome local storage.

  • No server storage of email content or attachments
  • No advertising or behavioral analytics
  • No personalized ads or sale of user data
  • Complies with the Chrome Web Store Limited Use requirements
